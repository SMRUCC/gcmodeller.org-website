Open Source by Nature

GCModeller is fully open source, with code hosted on GitHub. We believe transparency and community contributions are essential for building trustworthy scientific software.

Life Science in the Loop

From pathogen virulence pathways to multi‑omics analysis of bacterial stress responses, GCModeller is grounded in real‑world life science problems. We integrate genomics, transcriptomics, and metabolomics into a unified virtual cell framework.

Industrial‑Grade CAD for Biology

Inspired by engineering CAD tools, GCModeller provides a robust, scriptable toolchain for designing, simulating, and debugging cellular systems. With .NET and Rust at its core, it’s designed for stability, reproducibility, and performance at scale.

Virtual Cells, Real Insights

Build whole‑cell models, simulate their behavior, and analyze multi‑omics outputs to understand emergent phenotypes. GCModeller supports both forward engineering of synthetic genomes and reverse engineering of natural ones.

How GCModeller Works

Design & Annotate

Define your cellular model by annotating cell components and assembling a complete data model. GCModeller provides annotation protocols and a structured environment for building whole‑cell blueprints.

Simulate & Compute

Run simulated calculations on your annotated cell model. Store simulation trajectories and multi‑omics states for later analysis, enabling in‑silico experiments under genetic or environmental perturbations.

Analyze & Redesign

Analyze simulation outputs with built‑in tools for omics data interpretation, then refine your model. Export designed cell models to full‑length genome sequences for synthetic or experimental validation.
